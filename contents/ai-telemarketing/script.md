## 🇬🇧 BẢN TIẾNG ANH (ENGLISH VOICE SCRIPT — GENERAL, ANY INDUSTRY)

### Metadata
- **Target Duration:** 180 seconds (3.0 minutes)
- **Total Word Count:** ~380 words (Pacing: ~126 words/minute)

### Spoken Script

#### 1. Hook (00:00 – 00:12)
> *"How do you respond to a prospective customer at the 'golden moment', right when their interest in your product or service is at its absolute peak?"*

#### 2. Context & Client Request (00:12 – 01:00)
> *"Recently, CyberK took on a challenge from a business running an appointment-based service model. They were running an ad campaign for a new product package, and customer inquiries were pouring in continuously. However, the problem was that customers are most responsive in the first few minutes after submitting a form. If the consulting team was busy during peak hours and only called back two to three hours later, the customer's interest had cooled down, or they had already gone with another provider. The client asked CyberK to set up an automated voice solution that calls back instantly, conducts a natural conversation to qualify needs, and books an appointment directly with the right specialist."*

#### 3. Implementation Steps & System Workflow (01:00 – 01:50)
> *"To solve this, CyberK's first step was ingesting all the business's operational data, consultation scripts, and product or service information to train the automated voice assistant. Next, we established an automated workflow: When a customer fills out a form on the Landing Page, a Webhook API instantly receives the lead and triggers the automated system to dial the customer's mobile number in under 60 seconds. The voice assistant greets the customer politely, qualifies their needs and preferred service, and asks for their available time slots."*

#### 4. Core Challenge: Double Booking (01:50 – 02:20)
> *"However, the biggest hurdle was Double Booking. Imagine two different customers conversing with the automated system at the same time and booking the exact same time slot with the same specialist. If both customers show up only to find they were scheduled simultaneously, the service experience becomes highly unprofessional and severely damages the business's brand reputation."*

#### 5. CyberK Technical Solution & CTA (02:20 – 03:00)
> *"To eliminate this bottleneck completely, CyberK integrated a two-way calendar sync with Google Calendar and internal management software. Before suggesting any available time slot to the customer, the system automatically queries the specialist's real-time schedule. The moment the customer confirms a time, the system immediately locks that slot in the database using a unique identifier. This completely eliminates the risk of double booking.*
>
> *If you're a business owner or executive — in real estate, education, beauty & wellness, or professional services — looking to automate customer response and intelligent appointment booking, contact CyberK today to start the conversation."*
