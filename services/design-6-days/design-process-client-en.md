# Cyberk – 6-Day Design Process (client guide)

> From your idea to a product ready to build in 6 working days.

Source of truth: [cyberk-design-process-en.html](./cyberk-design-process-en.html) (8 A4 pages, with illustrations).

---

## 4 steps in 6 working days

Each step ends with a gate where you review, comment and approve before we move on.

01. **Discovery · Day 01**: Research similar products and users, then map your user-flow wireframes
02. **Prototype · Day 02**: Review a moodboard and 3 mockup options with our designer's recommendation
03. **Design System · Day 03**: Adapt a proven design system into core components so devs and AI build faster
04. **App Design · Days 04–06**: Complete all main user flows and edge cases: offline, empty, loading and error states

---

## Day 01 · Discovery
*Right problem, right flow*

_Illustration: research card → wireframe phone → wireframe list screen_

**What we do**
- Research similar products and competitors in your market
- Define your target user persona and core needs
- Map your user flows and wireframe key screens to align from the first meeting

**What you get**
- Reference list of similar products with links & screenshots
- Target user persona profile
- User-flow wireframes to confirm the right flow

**What we need from you:** Your product brief, business goals, and confirmation on the user-flow wireframes.

**Approval gate:** User flows & wireframes confirmed

---

## Day 02 · Prototype
*Test the look on a few screens*

_Illustration: moodboard + two coloured key screens_

**What we do**
- Build a visual moodboard (colour, typography and imagery) with clear rationale
- Design 3 distinct mockup options on key screens
- Provide design reasoning and our recommendation so you can choose with confidence

**What you get**
- Moodboard with design rationale
- 3 mockup choices with rationale & designer's suggestion

**What we need from you:** Choose the direction that best fits your brand — if none feels right yet, we loop back to Discovery.

**Approval gate:** Visual direction selected

---

## Day 03 · Design System
*One rulebook, faster build*

_Illustration: design system board: colours, type scale, components_

**What we do**
- Select a proven existing design system that fits your product and tech stack
- Build the core reusable components (buttons, forms, cards, navigation)
- Stress-test contrast, text scaling and touch targets, structured so AI tools and devs code fast

**What you get**
- Selected design system with clear rationale
- Core component library in Figma, ready for devs & AI tools
- Stress-test verification results

**What we need from you:** Nothing required. You are welcome to review and comment.

**Approval gate:** Design system & components ready

---

## Days 04–06 · App Design
*No screen left unfinished*

_Illustration: three edge-case screens: no internet, empty, error_

**What we do**
- Complete every screen across your main user flows
- Design the edge cases most apps forget: empty states, loading, offline and errors
- Stress-test layouts with long text, large numbers and real content
- Prepare clean Figma handoff files with spacing and interaction notes

**What you get**
- All main user-flow screens, including edge cases
- End-to-end interactive flow in Figma
- Clean, developer-ready handoff files

**What we need from you:** Review each screen and send us the final wording.

**Approval gate:** Design signed off for development

---

## Beyond the first 6 days

In 6 working days, you have a complete core design ready so developers can build in parallel without waiting. After day 6, your designer stays on the project with you.

1. **First 6 working days — Core design ready:** We dedicate the first 6 working days to your core flows, visual direction, design system and main screens. By day 6 you have a complete design ready to review, and our developers have everything they need to code in parallel.
2. **After day 6 — We stay with you:** Real products always surface new details once code is running. Our designer does not step away after day 6 — as you test live builds, we stay with the project to refine UX/UI from your feedback until release.
