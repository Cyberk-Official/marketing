# Source Code Rescue — Headline cho quảng cáo và email

Người đọc: CEO, quản lý, C-level công ty vừa và nhỏ. Họ không đọc được code, không hiểu team công nghệ đang vướng gì, và team dev thường trễ deadline kèm nhiều lý do.

Mỗi headline là một câu mở cho quảng cáo Meta, tiêu đề email hoặc dòng H1 trên landing page. Chia theo góc nỗi đau để dễ chọn và A/B test.

---

## A. Deadline và ước lượng

1. When your dev team says it needs two more weeks, how do you know if that is true?
2. Your last three releases were late. Do you know why?
3. How would you check a three-week estimate if you can't read the code?
4. Every sprint ends with a new reason for the delay. Who checks those reasons?
5. A feature planned for two weeks shipped three months later. What happened in between?

## B. Không hiểu team công nghệ

6. Your CTO explained the delay for twenty minutes. How much of it could you repeat to your board?
7. You pay your dev team every month. Can you tell whether the work is good?
8. What would you ask your developers if you knew which questions to ask?
9. If your lead developer quit tomorrow, who else would understand the code?

## C. Lỗi lặp lại

10. Why does a bug your team fixed in May come back in July?
11. How many days a month does your team spend on urgent fixes?
12. Each release is followed by a few days of fixes. Is that normal for a team your size?

## D. Chi phí và viết lại

13. Your team wants to rewrite the system. Is that the cheapest option you have?
14. How much of your development budget goes into fixing old code?

## E. Bảo mật

15. Could a logged-in user in your app read another user's orders?
16. Where does your app store its passwords and payment keys, and who can see them?

## F. Code thừa kế và gọi vốn

17. An investor wants a technical review next month. What will it find?
18. The agency that built your app has moved on. What did they leave in the code?
19. Part of your product was built with Cursor or Lovable. Has anyone read that code since?

## G. Dẫn bằng offer

20. A Senior Developer can review your code in 48 hours, for $500.


## H. Làm lại hay sửa

21. Before you pay to rebuild your app from scratch, find out which parts actually need it.
22. Your team says starting over would be faster. Has anyone checked that?
23. The quote to rebuild your app came in at six months. How much of that work is necessary?
24. Your new agency wants to throw away the old code. Is it really that bad?
25. Fixing the parts that break may cost less than rebuilding everything. Do you know which is cheaper?
26. You already paid for this app once. Find out what can be kept before paying for it again.

## I. Vấn đề của vibe code

27. Your app was built with Cursor in a few weeks. Has anyone read the code since?
28. Lovable got you to launch. Who is going to maintain the code it wrote?
29. The AI wrote each feature without looking at the ones before it. Has anyone checked how they fit together?
30. Is your API key sitting in the code that every visitor's browser downloads?
31. Every new prompt fixes one screen and breaks another. How long can that go on?
32. Your app works in the demo. Will it still work with ten thousand users?
33. If the person who prompted the app leaves, can anyone else change it?
34. The app was built in a weekend. Why does every change now take two weeks?


## J. Lời hứa trước → sau (thông điệp, chưa phải câu mô tả dịch vụ)

Trễ deadline
- From "two more weeks" every sprint to estimates you can check against a written report.
- Stop accepting delay reasons you can't verify. Within 48 hours you get a written list of what is actually slowing your team down.
- From status meetings full of terms you don't know to a report you can read in one sitting and discuss with your team.

Buggy
- Bugs that come back every month get fixed once, with a test that catches them if they return.
- From a few days of urgent fixes after every release to releases your team can ship on an ordinary weekday. *(cần xác nhận Implement làm được)*

Laggy
- From "the app is slow and nobody knows why" to a list of the exact screens causing it, with the time it takes to fix each one.
- Find out in 48 hours why your app slows down as it grows, and what it costs to fix.

Costly
- Before you pay for a full rebuild, see whether fixing costs less. The report puts both numbers side by side.
- From a cloud bill that grows every month to a clear view of what is driving it. *(chỉ đúng nếu audit có xem hạ tầng cloud)*

Gộp
- Every problem in the report gets fixed and checked against a condition both sides agreed on before the work started.

3. Diagnosis and treatment for code that keeps breaking.

Lấy lại quyền kiểm soát

6. Understand your code without learning to read it.
7. Know what your dev team knows.
8. An outside review of your code, written so you can read it.

Sửa và cứu

Vibe code

15. Your AI-built app, reviewed and fixed by engineers.

Kết quả

## Ghi chú khi chọn

- Nhóm A và B đánh trúng nhất người đọc đã mô tả (trễ deadline, không hiểu team). Nên test trước.
- Câu 6 và 9 có thể gây cảm giác "đổ lỗi cho CTO". Nếu chạy cho tệp có cả CTO đọc, cân nhắc bỏ.
- Câu 15 cụ thể nhất nhưng hợp với email hơn quảng cáo, vì người đọc cần dừng lại nghĩ.
- Câu 20 không phải câu hỏi. Dùng cho retargeting, khi người xem đã biết dịch vụ.
- Các con số trong câu 2, 3, 5 và 10 là bối cảnh minh hoạ, không phải số liệu đo được, nên không vi phạm §8.
- Nhóm H hợp với người đang cầm báo giá làm lại từ agency khác. Câu 21 và 26 dùng tốt cho email cold outreach.
- Nhóm I nhắm đúng tệp đã vibe code. Câu 34 gom được cả hai nỗi đau (vibe code và trễ deadline) nên là ứng viên test mạnh.
- Câu 30 dùng thuật ngữ "API key". Với người đọc non-tech, đổi thành "payment key" hoặc "password" nếu cần.
- Nhóm K: câu 1 và 2 khớp với tên gói Health Check, tạo được một hệ ẩn dụ xuyên suốt (Health Check → Audit → Rescue). Câu 11 nói thẳng nhất với người đang định làm lại.
- Không dùng "daily health check": dịch vụ hiện chưa có gói kiểm tra định kỳ. Nếu sau này có (ví dụ gói theo tháng), đó là một tagline tốt.
