Who we serve

Why speed matters

Why speed matters

Speed is the main reason clients choose Cyberk. Each step leads to the next.

1FastWe ship in days, not months, on a base that is already built.
2Lower costFewer months of work means a smaller bill.
3Less sunk costLess money spent on ideas before they are tested with real users.
4Launch earlyA product in users’ hands sooner, with real feedback to learn from.
5Revenue earlyPaying users start sooner, so the product begins to earn.
6Raise soonerA live product with users and revenue is a stronger case for investors.

Zero to Product

Zero to Product

From $25,00030 days · 30 days of Care included · 14 days: +$7,500

For founders starting a new product. Our main offer and the best place to start.

Signals to listen for

  • Has an idea, but no product yet
  • Plans to raise money, or has a demo day coming
  • Has a limited budget or a deadline
  • Expects building to take many months

Why it fits

  • Shipping fast saves money: version 1 in 30 days
  • Need it sooner? Version 1 in 14 days for +$7,500
  • Fewer months of work means less cost and less sunk cost
  • The client sees the running product in a short demo every day
  • Code, infrastructure and design files belong to the client

6-day Design

6-day Design

$4,500Per project · 6 working days

For clients who want to try us first, and for repeat clients and serial founders who want to test a new idea before writing code.

Signals to listen for

  • Wants to see our work before committing
  • A repeat client or serial founder with a new idea
  • Wants to research the market and test the idea before building
  • Has no clear scope or design yet
  • Has not approved a budget for the full build

Why it fits

  • A small, low-risk start: $4,500 and 6 working days
  • Test a new idea with market research and a full design before paying for code
  • The client gets user flows, a design system and every main screen in Figma
  • The client judges us on real work, not on a pitch
  • The design is ready to build, so Zero to Product can start right after

Care+

Care+

$1,000/moMinimum 6 months · first 30 days included with Zero to Product

For live products on pause: waiting for funding, changing direction, or not ready to grow yet.

Signals to listen for

  • Version 1 is live, but new development stops
  • Waiting for a funding round
  • Wants to rethink or change the idea
  • Worried the product will break while no one works on it

Why it fits

  • Very low cost: $1,000 a month
  • Someone still looks after the product: alerts, reports and bug fixes
  • The client hears about issues from us first
  • The product stays healthy, so building can restart any time

Tech Labs

Tech Labs

From $3,000/moPer developer · Senior $5,000 · Fullstack $3,000

For live products that keep growing and need a reliable tech team that works like the client’s own in-house team.

Signals to listen for

  • Priorities can change every day
  • The same team keeps the system running and the client keeps 100% of the code
