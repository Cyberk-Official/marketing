## V. Special Case Study: Lei Jun (雷军) & Xiaomi — The Pinnacle of Founder-Led Marketing
*This is a classic case of using the founder's personal brand to directly drive product sales and shape the brand narrative.*

### Lei Jun (雷军)
* **Positioning:** "People's Entrepreneur" | Super IP representing the entire Xiaomi ecosystem | Veteran tech figure utilizing New Media.
* **Predecessor & Development Process:**
  * A monument in the Chinese tech industry. Graduated from Wuhan University, was one of the early engineers at Kingsoft, and rose to become its CEO before its successful IPO.
  * In 2010, he co-founded Xiaomi with the philosophy of high-spec, affordable smartphones for everyone.
  * Before exploding on Douyin/TikTok, Lei Jun was already highly popular on Weibo and active on traditional media. However, his biggest Self-Media turning point came when preparing for the final startup project of his life: **Xiaomi SU7 electric vehicle** (2021-2024). He built a personal Douyin channel to directly interact and unlock the entire car manufacturing process for users.
* **Storytelling via Short Video & Livestreaming:**
  * **The Everyman Persona:** Unlike traditional, detached corporate executives, Lei Jun appears on Douyin in simple clothes, sharing his breakfast, biking to work, or showing his daily outfits. This narrows the gap between the billionaire and the average consumer.
  * **Self-deprecation:** When his English speech in India was turned into the viral mock song "Are you OK?", instead of suing or deleting it, he bought the song rights and played it in Xiaomi Mi Home stores. Lei Jun turned a mocking meme into a powerful branding weapon to win public affection.
  * **Hero's Journey & The SU7 Campaign:** He framed the Xiaomi SU7 project as a high-stakes, life-or-death challenge — "the last startup project" of his life. Short videos focused on him learning professional track racing, drifting the car himself, or supervising the battery nail-penetration stress test. Viewers did not just buy a car; they bought Lei Jun's persistence and dream.
  * **Transparency & Direct Response:** Lei Jun regularly answers comments in short videos. During the first batch of SU7 handovers, he personally bowed and opened the car doors for customers. The video immediately went viral, redefining customer service standards in the auto industry.
* **Matrix Strategy:**
  * Main channels: *雷军 (Lei Jun)* on Douyin (over 25 million followers), Weibo, and international TikTok/X accounts.
  * Collaborates closely with Xiaomi's official account matrix (Xiaomi Phone, Xiaomi EV, and other Xiaomi executives who are encouraged to establish personal IPs as satellite channels).
* **Core Business Lesson:**
  * **Founder-Led Marketing:** When a founder builds an authentic, massive personal brand, they can bypass millions in traditional ad spend. The historic, record-breaking sales of the SU7 were almost entirely driven by Lei Jun's organic campaign and his loyal fan base.
* **Douyin Link:** [Search Lei Jun](https://www.douyin.com/search/雷军)
