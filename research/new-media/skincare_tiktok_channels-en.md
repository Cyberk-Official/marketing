# List of Famous Skincare TikTok Channels (Global & Vietnam)

Below is a compilation of the most famous TikTok channels (SkinTok) in the skincare industry, divided into two main groups: International and Vietnam. These channels represent very different content formats and personal brand positioning (IP positioning), ranging from board-certified dermatologists to battle-tested KOCs.

---

## I. Outstanding International Channels

### 1. DermDoctor (Dr. Muneeb Shah)
* **Account:** `@dermdoctor` (18 million+ followers)
* **Positioning:** Friendly, humorous, and approachable professional dermatologist.
* **Content Style:**
  * **React & Stitch:** This is his most signature format. He stitches with videos of users popping pimples, experiencing unusual skin conditions, or trying bizarre viral skincare hacks, then offers medical advice from a professional perspective.
  * **Simplifying Medicine:** Explaining clinical terms in everyday language, helping viewers quickly identify skin conditions without needing to visit a clinic immediately.
* **IP Lesson:** Build supreme credibility using academic background but reach viewers with high empathy and entertainment value.

### 2. Skincare by Hyram (Hyram Yarbro)
* **Account:** `@skincarebyhyram` (6 million+ followers)
* **Positioning:** Skinfluencer - A smart, honest consumer advocate focused on core values.
* **Content Style:**
  * **Brutally Honest Reviews:** Direct and transparent evaluations of products from major brands or celebrity skincare lines.
  * **Minimalist Skincare (Skinimalism):** Campaigning against overloading the skin with too many steps, focusing on gentle ingredients, environmental sustainability, and affordable pricing.
* **IP Lesson:** You do not need a medical degree to become an expert if you stand on the side of consumer interests and analyze products based on formulation chemistry.

### 3. Lab Muffin Beauty Science (Dr. Michelle Wong)
* **Account:** `@labmuffinbeautyscience`
* **Positioning:** Cosmetic Chemist.
* **Content Style:**
  * **Decoding the Science:** Focusing on dissecting the chemical mechanisms behind skincare products (e.g., how sunscreen filters work at a molecular level, the truth about paraben preservatives).
  * **Debunking Myths:** Using scientific empirical evidence to dispel groundless rumors about toxic cosmetics.
* **IP Lesson:** Position as an absolute technical expert, using data and empirical studies to convince the most demanding customer base.

## II. Outstanding Vietnamese Channels

### 1. Vo Ha Linh
* **Account:** `@halinhofficial` (4.5 million+ followers)
* **Positioning:** "Review Warrior" (Chiến thần review) - The gold standard for cosmetic truth in Vietnam.
* **Content Style:**
  * **Brutally Honest Real-world Reviews:** Direct criticism or praise without hesitation. Praising outstanding products and exposing low-quality ones, even from major brands.
  * **High Authenticity:** Personally testing products for long periods on her own skin before making final conclusions.
* **IP Lesson:** Absolute user trust is built on refusing sponsorships to speak untruths. Honesty is the highest conversion weapon.

### 2. Call Me Duy (Vu Duy)
* **Account:** `@callmeduy` (500K+ followers)
* **Positioning:** Ingredient Formulation Expert & Advanced Routine Designer (Winner of KOC Vietnam 2022).
* **Content Style:**
  * **INCI (Ingredient List) Analysis:** Inspecting every substance in cosmetics to explain why a product is effective or irritating.
  * **Routine Personalization:** Designing detailed skincare regimens for specific targets (treatment skin, acne-prone skin, recovery after low-quality peeling creams).
* **IP Lesson:** Attract educated consumers by equipping them with the knowledge to evaluate cosmetics themselves rather than buying based on advertisements.

### 3. Dr. Do Quoc Tuan
* **Account:** `@drdotuan` (200K+ followers)
* **Positioning:** Young, Gen Z, trend-aware dermatologist.
* **Content Style:**
  * **Humorous Skits:** Weaving common skincare mistakes among youth into short, fun, and engaging sketches.
  * **Pocket Medical Advice:** Acne-clearing and oily skin tips summarized in highly memorable 30-second clips.
* **IP Lesson:** Rejuvenate medical knowledge to reach students - the group with the highest rate of acne issues but limited budgets.

### 4. Pharmacist Tong Duc
* **Account:** `@duocsitongduc` (500K+ followers)
* **Positioning:** Clinical cosmetic pharmacist expert.
* **Content Style:**
  * **Scientific Regimen Consulting:** Guiding users on how to combine strong active ingredients (Retinol, BHA, Vitamin C) safely at home.
  * **Direct Q&A:** Answering user questions directly from comments via detailed video explanations.
* **IP Lesson:** Become a trusted companion in the user's journey of self-treating skin at home.

### 5. Happy Skin (Emmi Hoang)
* **Account:** `@happyskinvn` (400K+ followers)
* **Positioning:** Long-standing, credible channel focusing on skincare and elegant lifestyle.
* **Content Style:**
  * **Routine & Lifestyle:** Sharing anti-aging skincare routines and deep skin treatments combined with healthy eating and living habits.
  * **Clinic Experience:** Reviewing high-tech beauty clinic treatments (laser, micro-needling) in real-world scenarios.
* **IP Lesson:** Target mature customers (25 - 40 years old) with high income who care about anti-aging and comprehensive skincare.
