# CYBERK SHORT VIDEO STRATEGY (PAAST & VCL FRAMEWORKS)
## A Guide to Measuring, Evaluating, and Optimizing Short Video Virality Probability

This document defines **Cyberk's** short video production strategy based on New-Media methodology, applying two practical models: **PAAST** (Viewer Psychological Journey) and **VCL** (Technical Execution Framework) to standardize the process of evaluating and optimizing the virality probability of each video before and after publication.

---

## 1. Channel Essence & IP Positioning

Cyberk does not position itself as a traditional outsourcing dev-house, nor as a generic tech review channel.
* **IP Positioning:** **"The Pragmatic Innovation Lab"** — a place to share the raw truth behind source code, real-world software engineering experiences, and practical AI applications.
* **Content Creed:** **Show, Don't Tell** (Let viewers witness and draw their own conclusions through running code, real product demos, and debug logs).

---

## 2. The PAAST Model: Viewer Psychological Funnel for Cyberk Videos

The **PAAST** model is designed to convert casual short-video viewers into partners, developers within the community, or B2B clients of Cyberk.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ P - PREFER: Picked to watch due to "Pragmatic Truth"   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ A - ACTION: Immediate action (Click bio, grab code)    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ A - ACKNOWLEDGE: Empathize with key B2B pain points    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ S - STICK: Engage through journey series (Daily Log)   │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ T - TRUST: Build trust from real code & raw demos      │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### P — Prefer (Preference)
* **Insight:** Tech viewers are tired of pure theoretical lectures or chém gió buzzwords (AI buzzwords) that have no real-world application.
* **Execution:** Cyberk wins attention by opening with "just like real-world execution" scenes: *a buggy IDE screen, red error logs running down the terminal, or the actual file structure of a major project.*
* **Opening Hook:** *"Don't listen to them chém gió about AI Agents anymore, here is how it actually runs and here is why it crashed..."*

### A — Action (Conversion Action)
* **Goal:** Trigger immediate conversion actions to measure distribution effectiveness.
* **Execution:** The end of the video always presents a high-value, low-friction call-to-action (CTA):
  * *"I left this entire `docker-compose` configuration file in the link in bio, click to download and use immediately."*
  * *"Comment 'AI' below, my bot will automatically send you the Github repository link."*

### A — Acknowledge (Empathy/Understanding)
* **Insight:** B2B clients fear spending tens of thousands of dollars on outsourcing only to receive junk software or non-functional AI. Developers fear changing technology stacks and getting left behind.
* **Execution:** Empathize directly with real-world pain points:
  * *"We know you've lost money on promises that AI could do everything..."*
  * *"Every developer encounters this exact error at least once when configuring a database..."*

### S — Stick (Retention/Engagement)
* **Goal:** Retain viewers to return to the channel and follow long-term.
* **Execution:** Package videos into **Project Building Series (Build in Public)**. Viewers stick around due to curiosity about progress:
  * *Series: "90 Days of Automating Cyberk Office with AI Agents from Scratch."*
  * *Series: "Code Review Friday — Exposing the dumbest code lines draining server budgets."*

### T — Trust (Building Trust)
* **Goal:** Convert viewers into clients or brand advocates.
* **Execution:**
  * Show the running interface (demo) of the software/AI directly without edits or cuts.
  * The Founder/Tech Lead directly responds to the deepest technical critique comments from viewers using detailed video explanations.

## 3. The VCL Execution Framework (Visual - Content - Logic)

The **VCL** framework is the set of mandatory technical standards that the Cyberk production team must apply during filming and editing.

### V — Visual (Pragmatic Tech Visuals)
* **Signature Format:**
  * Close-ups of the IDE screen (VS Code/Cursor) typing code or terminal executing commands.
  * Over-the-shoulder camera angles showing the engineer typing on the keyboard with code glowing on screen, creating absolute authenticity.
  * Draw hand-sketched architecture diagrams or use Mermaid charts to visualize data flows instead of talking abstractly.
* **Graphics & Subtitles:** Subtitles use developer-friendly fonts (such as RobotoMono), large and clear, with key technical terms highlighted in Cyberk's signature green/blue colors.

### C — Content (1-1-1 Content Structure)
* **Core Formula:** **1 Video = 1 Specific Problem = 1 Practical Solution**. No bloating.
* **3 Core IP Storylines:**
  1. *Exposé/Trap Avoidance Series:* Expose basic security bugs, cloud spend traps, or the raw reality of integrating AI.
  2. *Founder's Journal (Build in Public):* The journey of solving real operations issues at Cyberk, hiring difficulties, or deal negotiations.
  3. *Tips & Tools Series:* Sharing extremely effective automation tools that Cyberk uses internally.
* **Content Ethics:** Absolutely no hype about technology, no sharing of buggy/non-functional code, and always keep client data and `.env` secure.

### L — Logic/Kỹ thuật (Distribution & Funnel System)
* **Posting Cadence:** Fixed 3-5 videos/week to train platform algorithms to identify target viewers (developers/tech enthusiasts/business owners).
* **Asset Restructuring:** 1 long vertical 60s video on TikTok/Reels $\rightarrow$ Recut into 3 short 15s videos focusing on hooks $\rightarrow$ Written into detailed articles on Gitbook/LinkedIn.
* **Funnel Tracking:** Every bio link/comment call must be tagged with UTM parameters to measure conversion accurately:
  * *Views $\rightarrow$ Bio Link Clicks $\rightarrow$ Document Downloads/Github Stars $\rightarrow$ Consultations.*

## 4. Video Scorecard (Before & After Publication)

To scientifically increase virality probability, each video must be audited against these two scorecards:

### 4.1. Pre-Publish Scorecard
*The reviewer (Content Lead/Founder) grades each criterion from 1-5. Videos achieving **12/15 points** or higher are approved for publication.*

| Criterion | Description | Score (1-5) |
|---|---|---|
| **1. First 3s Hook** | Does the first visual or statement grab developers' attention? (Buggy code, terminal executing, or a golden phrase?) | |
| **2. Usefulness (Useful)** | Does the viewer learn a specific solution they can apply immediately after watching? | |
| **3. Authenticity (Show, Don't Tell)** | Does the video show real-world actions (screen recording of typing code, app demo) instead of theory? | |
| **4. Call to Action (CTA)** | Is the CTA clear, easy, and motivating enough to pull the viewer into the funnel? | |
| **5. VCL Standardization** | Is the subtitle font RobotoMono? Is the audio clear and editing snappy without distractions? | |
| **TOTAL SCORE** | **Requirement: $\ge$ 12 points** | **/25** |

### 4.2. Post-Publish Metrics
*The Community & Data team updates metrics after 24h, 72h, and 7 days to evaluate algorithm resonance and adjust formats.*

| Metric | Minimum Target | Significance | How to Optimize if Target Missed |
|---|---|---|---|
| **3s Retention Rate** | $\ge$ 65% | Measures the strength of the opening Hook. | Adjust text overlay title or choose a more visually engaging opening shot. |
| **Completion Rate** | $\ge$ 20% | Measures the engaging nature (Interesting) of the video body. | Trim excess footage, speed up pacing, or break complex topics into mini-series. |
| **Save & Share Rate** | $\ge$ 4% | Measures the practical value (Useful) of the video. | Focus on sharing code templates, checklists, or easily saved practical tips. |
| **Bio Link CTR** | $\ge$ 1.5% | Measures the effectiveness of the Logic (Funnel conversion). | Simplify landing pages, clarify the value of the lead magnet/document. |
