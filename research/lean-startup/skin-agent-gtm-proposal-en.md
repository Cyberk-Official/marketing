# GO-TO-MARKET PROPOSAL: SKIN AGENT
## Short Video Brand & Customer Development Strategy (IP-Driven New Media)

> **Methodology:** New-Media | **Approach Model:** PAAST (Prefer → Action → Acknowledge → Stick → Trust) | **Execution Framework:** VCL (Visual – Content – Logic)
> **Reference Material:** China IP Case Studies (Douyin B2B, AI & Founder IP)

## 1. Context: User Behavior Has Changed — Old Branding Methods Are No Longer Effective

### 1.1. Today's User Behavior

**Users are tired of brands making self-assertions.** Claims like *"No. 1 Brand"*, *"Leading Water Purifier"*, or *"Trusted by Millions"* are no longer effective — they can even backfire by triggering the user's "this is an ad" defense mechanism. Today's users want to **witness the process, listen, observe, and draw their own conclusions.** The power to make conclusions has shifted from the brand to the viewer. Brands that make self-proclamations will be skipped; brands that present evidence will be watched.

**Entertainment behavior has shifted.** Users are leaving static Facebook feeds to find excitement on short video platforms (TikTok, Reels, Shorts). They no longer separate "entertainment time" and "product research time" — they **learn while being entertained (edutainment)**. Content that is both engaging and useful will capture that golden window of time; pure advertising content stands no chance.

Combining these two shifts results in one outcome: **the most effective communication channel today is the one that lets users witness for themselves — not the one that speaks loudest.**

### 1.2. The Problems with the KOL Booking Model

- **The more you invest, the more viral the KOL becomes → their price rises, and the brand loses control.** The booking paradox: it is the brand's budget that grows the KOL, only for the brand to pay a higher price for the next collaboration. All assets (followers, credibility, audience data) belong to the KOL — the brand is just renting, and once the money stops, everything stops.
- **Users are tired of and no longer trust KOL reviews.** When a single face reviews too many products, viewers immediately see it as disguised advertising. Trust — the only thing KOLs sell — is losing its value rapidly in its own market.

### 1.3. The Problems with Paid Advertising

**Platform costs are rising, and ad spend retains no value.** CPM/CPC increase every year while traffic quality decreases. More importantly: ads are a **rented attention** model — turn off the money, turn off the traffic. The spent budget does not accumulate into any asset for the brand. Run ads for 3 years, stop for 1 day, and the result goes back to 0.

### 1.4. Why Short Video (IP) is the Answer

**Short video is brand intellectual property (IP).** Unlike ads (renting) and KOLs (borrowing), every video a brand produces is a **permanently owned asset**: it continues to be distributed by algorithms, searched for, and builds trust — months and years after posting.

**Every short video is a 24/7 conversion engine.** A video that solves exactly one problem for the viewer will continuously pull new users into the funnel: watch → believe → install app — without paying an extra cent for distribution.

**Short video production costs are much cheaper than running ads, but the effects are long-term and cumulative.** An ad budget spent is gone; a video budget is an investment — 100 videos after 6 months are a library of assets working in parallel, compounding in value over time.

| Attribute | Paid Ads | KOL Booking | Short Video (Owned IP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nature | Renting attention | Borrowing trust | **Owning trust** |
| When spending stops | Traffic drops to 0 | Nothing remains | Assets continue working |
| Cost over time | Increases | Increases | Decreases per result |
| Control | Platform controlled | KOL controlled | **Brand controlled** |
| User trust | Low (known as ads) | Declining | High (watch, conclude yourself) |

> **Context Conclusion:** The game has shifted from *"who can buy the most attention"* to *"who can own the most trust"*. The entire strategy below is built for Skin Agent to become the **owner**, not the renter.

## 2. Executive Summary

Skin Agent does not sell an app. Skin Agent sells **peace of mind about skin** — something millions of young people are searching for in a skincare market cluttered with ads, KOL bookings, and misinformation.

The proposed strategy: **do not run product ads first — build storytelling IPs first.** The entire value of the company (AI technology, dermatological knowledge, transparency) will be packaged into **short video stories**, each video being an **Educational + Useful + Interesting** story that helps viewers solve a specific skin issue. When viewers solve a problem $\rightarrow$ they trust. When they trust $\rightarrow$ they download the app. When the app proves its effectiveness $\rightarrow$ they stay and recommend it.

This is precisely the path that Zhang Qi (12M followers in 1 month), Zhou Hongyi (360), Data Senior Sister (Beihang), and Lei Jun (Xiaomi SU7) have taken on Douyin — localized for the Vietnamese market on TikTok / Reels / YouTube Shorts.

**12-Month Objectives:**

| Phase | Timeline | Core Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0 → 1 (Test & Find Format) | Months 1–3 | Find 2–3 "algorithm-friendly" video formats, 30–50K followers on the main channel |
| 1 → 10 (Scale Main IP) | Months 4–8 | 150–300K followers, organic app installs $\ge$ 30% of total installs |
| 10 → 100 (Matrix & Conversion) | Months 9–12 | Matrix of 3–5 channels, private community $\ge$ 20K, measurable video $\rightarrow$ installs $\rightarrow$ retention funnel |

## 3. Company Essence: From Core Value to IP

### 3.1. What is Skin Agent (Essence, not features)

Product and market analysis shows that Skin Agent stands on 3 value pillars:

1. **Technology that sees what the naked eye cannot** — AI computer vision analyzing skin (acne, pores, pigmentation, wrinkles), trained specifically for Asian skin tones. This is the brand's "superpower."
2. **Personalization over generic advice** — morning/evening routines based on actual skin condition at any given time, checking product ingredient compatibility with the user's specific skin.
3. **Long-term companionship, not selling fear** — standardized skin journals over time, tracking real progress, connecting with experts when needed. No fear-mongering to make sales.

### 3.2. Conversion Principle: Company Value = Stories Told (IP)

Each value pillar must become a **repeatable story thread**:

| Company Value | Story Thread (Story IP) | Video Example |
|---|---|---|
| AI sees what the naked eye cannot | *"Skin Scanning with Skin Agent"* — real skin scans, real results series | "This person thought their skin was healthy, but the AI scanned 3 areas of severe dehydration — here's why" |
| Personalization | *"Where is your routine wrong?"* — dissecting real routines | "This girl used 8 skincare steps and her skin was still bad — the problem lies in step 2" |
| Companionship, no fear-mongering | *"90 Days with One Skin"* — evidence-based improvement journal | "Day 1 vs Day 90: What actually changes when you only do 3 things RIGHT" |

**Standard of a "successful" video (internal definition):** The viewer **learns one thing (Educational)** + **can apply it immediately (Useful)** + **watches to the end because it's engaging (Interesting)** + **solves a specific problem**. Lacking one of the four means the video is not successful, even with high views.

## 4. Chinese Case Studies: How They Changed

*Below are real stories selected from over 20 cases on Douyin — the most competitive and mature short video market in the world. We do not draw pre-packaged conclusions; we invite you to read their journeys, observe their secrets, and see for yourself what is repeating.*

### Case 1 — Zhang Qi (张琦): From 17-Year Offline Lecturer to 12 Million Followers in 1 Month

- **Before the change:** Worked 17–18 years as an offline lecturer and business consultant at Boshang Management — highly skilled, but only reached a few hundred students per class.
- **The change:** In early 2022, the organization decided to digitize knowledge, selected her as the representative IP face, and placed a professional team behind her to handle scripting, filming, and account matrix operations.
- **Results:** 12 million followers in the first month, first-month revenue of 10 million RMB (~$35 billion VND), total followers across the web exceeded 100 million.
- **Her secret:** No lectures — she spoke in sharp "golden phrases" hitting the pain points of small and medium business owners, with energy filling the screen; backed by a matrix of dozens of accounts amplifying the same message.

### Case 2 — Zhou Hongyi (周鸿祎, CEO of 360 Group): The Tech Billionaire Choosing a "Grassroots" Style

- **Before the change:** An Internet pioneer in China, famous for his aggressive competition during the PC era. The 360 brand needed to be repositioned in the AI wave.
- **The change:** In 2023–2024, he personally stepped to the front lines as a KOL under the nickname "Red Shirt Uncle" — promoting his company's AI products for free.
- **Results:** Over 16 million followers on Douyin, over 20 million across the web. In 2025, his video series "Child Smartwatch Safety Guide" reached over 81 million views — driving the entire industry to issue new safety standards.
- **His secret:** Not preachy, not condescending, not cold — approachable to the point of being "simple/grassroots." His most-viewed video didn't sell anything: it protected the viewers' children.

### Case 3 — Data Senior Sister (数据学姐, Beihang Associate Professor): Academy Stepping Down to Everyday Life

- **Before the change:** PhD, Tsinghua Postdoc, Beihang Associate Professor — top-tier knowledge locked inside university lecture halls and scientific journals.
- **The change:** She brought data science to short videos, driven by a desire to help average workers who feared being replaced by AI.
- **Results:** Became a highly trusted AI popularization expert with a high trust rating in the industry and her own knowledge channel matrix.
- **Her secret:** Never start with theory. Every video is a problem an average person faces: *"How to use AI to write reports?"* — academic knowledge only appears when it is actively solving the viewer's problem.

### Case 4 — Punk Chu (朋克周): A Filming Method Becoming a Whole Brand

- **Before the change:** Spent years writing tech articles and digital culture critiques — good writing, but anonymous to the public.
- **The change:** When ChatGPT/Midjourney exploded, he shifted to short videos and created a filming style no one had done before: close-up shots of a finger pointing at a computer screen running AI, combined with fast-paced voiceover narration.
- **Results:** 7.33 million followers on Douyin; that filming style became his "signature" — viewers recognize his video within half a second of scrolling.
- **His secret:** He doesn't own AI news (anyone can report news) — he owns **the way of telling**. The signature format is an asset no one can replicate.

### Case 5 — Lei Jun (雷军, Xiaomi): Breaking Car Sales Records with Almost Zero Ad Spend

- **Before the change:** Already a tech legend, famous on Weibo and traditional media — but Xiaomi was about to enter its biggest gamble: the SU7 electric vehicle.
- **The change:** He launched a personal Douyin channel and unlocked the entire car-making process for the public to witness: learning track racing to understand cars, drifting them himself, supervising battery nail-penetration tests. He told the story of the SU7 as "his life's last entrepreneurial project." When the "Are you OK?" mock meme went viral, he bought the song rights and played it in Xiaomi stores. On the first delivery day, he bowed and opened doors for customers himself.
- **Results:** Over 25 million followers on Douyin; SU7 sales broke records with almost zero traditional advertising budget — all driven by organic self-media and his fan community.
- **His secret:** He doesn't sell car specs. He lets people **witness the process** and draw their own conclusions. SU7 buyers said they bought Lei Jun's persistence and dream.

### Case 6 — Captain Heiba & Moushi Culture Ecosystem: The Operators Behind Nearly 100 IPs

- **Before the change:** Heiba was a project manager at traditional ad agencies in Hangzhou; Moushi Culture (Jitang Media) started with traditional TV professionals in Zhejiang.
- **The change:** They never bet on a single channel. Heiba spent 4 years behind the scenes operating IPs for nearly 100 founders; Moushi built an entire ecosystem: Cui Lei (14M), Commercial Memo (23M), Nange Has Finance (13M), Gao Gailun (16M)...
- **Results:** Heiba accumulated over 10 billion views across the web; Moushi became one of the strongest knowledge MCNs in China.
- **His secret:** Channel matrix — multiple channels, multiple voices, same core values — and private traffic funnels to avoid platform dependency. Notably, the creed repeated across all Moushi IPs is *"no fear-mongering, no fabrication."*

### And a Counter-Case — San Ge (参哥): The Cost of Over-Aggression

Rose from sea cucumber trading to a million-follower matrix and a private consulting club costing 80,000 RMB/seat. However, his aggressive and extreme approach led to his main channel being **banned** and faced severe controversies. In this document, he is the only one who lost his channel — and the only one who chased growth at all costs.

## 7. Content Strategy: 4 Content Pillars

Each pillar = a story thread. Proposed weekly ratio for 5 videos/week:

| Pillar | Ratio | Role in PAAST | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 — Skin Scanning & Truth** | 40% | Prefer + Action | Scanning real people's skin, reading results, pointing out surprises. The channel's signature format. |
| **P2 — Solving Skin Math** | 30% | Action + Acknowledge | Each video solves exactly 1 problem: "Why forehead acne?", "Order of applying these 4 products?" |
| **P3 — Consumer Protection** | 15% | Acknowledge + Trust | Exposing dangerous trends, reading ingredient lists, warning against ad traps (following Zhou Hongyi: no fear-mongering, no fabrication). |
| **P4 — Journey & People** | 15% | Stick + Trust | Founder tells product building stories, users tell 90-day stories, behind-the-scenes AI training. |

**Standard Script Structure (30–60 seconds per video):**

1. **Hook (0–3s):** Golden phrase or eye-catching skin scan visual to trigger curiosity.
2. **Problem (3–10s):** Name the exact pain point of a specific persona.
3. **Story/Evidence (10–40s):** Tell it through a real case or real AI scan results.
4. **Immediate Solution (40–55s):** Something the viewer can do tonight, WITHOUT the app.
5. **Loop Open (55–60s):** "There are 2 more areas the AI detected — next video" + soft CTA.

> Golden Rule: **The value given away for free must be large enough that viewers feel guilty if they don't follow.** The app only appears as a tool in the story, not the main character of the advertisement.

## 8. The PAAST Model: Customer Journey via Short Video

PAAST is the measurement backbone of the entire campaign — each stage has its main content types and KPIs:

### P — Prefer (Chosen to watch among thousands of videos)
- **Task:** Win the first 3 seconds, win the recommendation algorithm.
- **Content:** Golden phrase hooks, eye-catching scan visuals, instant-recognition signature format.
- **KPI:** View count $\ge$ 50K/video by Month 3+, first 3s retention $\ge$ 70%, mid-video retention $\ge$ 50%.

### A — Action (Viewers act)
- **Task:** Turn passive viewers into active interactors: comment skin condition, save the video, follow instructions, click the link to download the app.
- **Content:** Challenge-based CTAs ("Take a photo of your skin tomorrow morning and compare it to this"), open-ended questions at the end of the video, mini-series forcing follow-ups.
- **KPI:** Save rate $\ge$ 3%, comment rate $\ge$ 1%, App Store CTR $\ge$ 1.5% on videos with CTA.

### A — Acknowledge (Acknowledge value & expertise)
- **Task:** Viewers acknowledge that "this channel is right, they have real expertise."
- **Content:** Videos with advisory doctors, citing research papers, correcting common misconceptions, having the courage to say "product X is unnecessary for your skin" (even when losing affiliate revenue).
- **KPI:** Ratio of positive comments/consultation requests increases monthly, P3 videos shared $\ge$ 2%.

### S — Stick (Stay, return, build habits)
- **Task:** Turn viewers into loyal followers, and app users into daily active users.
- **Content:** Periodic series with fixed schedules ("Skin Scanning Wednesday", "Trend Debunking Sunday"), 90-day journals of fixed characters, private Zalo/FB communities receiving exclusive content.
- **KPI:** Followers returning to watch $\ge$ 40%, App D30 retention improves quarterly, private community grows weekly.

### T — Trust (Trust $\rightarrow$ refer $\rightarrow$ defend brand)
- **Task:** Customers become storytellers for the brand.
- **Content:** UGC before/after with authenticated app data, founder directly and transparently responding to negative feedback (Lei Jun model: bowing to open car doors — translated to "founder calling users experiencing bugs directly"), periodic Q&A livestreams.
- **KPI:** $\ge$ 20% of new installs come from referral/organic search of the brand name by Month 12, NPS $\ge$ 50.

## 9. Execution Framework: VCL (Visual – Content – Logic)

### V — Visual (Eye recognition in 0.5 seconds)
- **Signature format:** Split-screen [AI scan results | real face], finger pointing to the skin areas on screen (Punk Chu style).
- **Color palette & Typography:** 1 brand main color + clean background styled as a "friendly lab"; large subtitles, keywords highlighted in brand color.
- **Human face:** Maximum of 2 fixed host faces in the first phase — audiences connect with people, not logos.
- **Standardized assets:** Intro-outro $\le$ 1.5s, shared CapCut/Premiere templates so that all editors produce consistent videos.

### C — Content (Stories are the product)
- **Content bank before shooting:** 100 golden phrases + 50 real questions from comments/skincare groups + 30 real skin cases (with consent) = enough material for 3 months.
- **Formula: 1 video = 1 problem = 1 solution.** Do not overload. Chop large problems into series.
- **Character roles:** expert host (knowledge), founder (journey), real user (evidence) — three storytelling voices woven together to create depth like the Moushi Culture ecosystem.
- **Content ethics (mandatory):** Do not scaremonger skin diseases, do not promise medical results, always include a disclaimer "does not replace doctor diagnosis," all before/after cases are real with app data.

### L — Logic/Kỹ thuật (Algorithm, data, system)
- **Posting cadence:** 5 videos/week on the main channel, testing A/B time slots in the first 4 weeks, then fixing them.
- **Batch testing:** Test a single variable each week (hook / length / format) — measure, keep the winner, discard the loser (Tian Nuo Lao Wu's data-driven spirit).
- **Video SEO:** Title + subtitles + hashtags cover actual search queries ("oily acne skin", "routine for beginners", "how to read ingredients").
- **End-to-end measurable funnel:** Video $\rightarrow$ link bio/deep link with UTM $\rightarrow$ App Store $\rightarrow$ in-app events (first scan, D7, D30). Weekly dashboard: which video brings installs, which install brings retention.
- **Matrix & Reuse:** 1 original video $\rightarrow$ recut for Reels/Shorts $\rightarrow$ long version for YouTube $\rightarrow$ carousel for Facebook. Produce once, distribute five times.
- **Paid fuels, organic sails:** Only boost videos that have proven good organic performance (high view/save rate) — do not burn money on videos not yet validated by the algorithm.

## 10. 12-Month Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Foundation & Test (Months 1–3)
- Weeks 1–2: Finalize Brand Story Bible (golden phrases, character lines, signature format, VCL guideline), cast host, set up mini studio.
- Weeks 3–12: Produce 5 videos/week on main channel + 2 videos/week on founder channel; 1 A/B experiment per week; build private group from day one (every video invites users to the group).
- **Phase transition milestone:** $\ge$ 2 formats achieve an average of 50K views, 30–50K followers, UTM funnel measures the first install from a video.

### Phase 2 — Scale Main IP (Months 4–8)
- Focus 70% of production resources on winning formats; launch periodic series with fixed schedules; advisory doctors appear weekly.
- Founder increases cadence: behind-the-scenes product, public response to users, 1 Q&A livestream/month.
- Launch UGC program: 90-day users receive rewards for sharing skin journals (with medical review).
- **Milestones:** 150–300K followers, $\ge$ 30% organic installs from social, private community $\ge$ 10K.

### Phase 3 — Matrix & Deep Conversion (Months 9–12)
- Replicate formats to 2–3 niche channels (teen acne / pigmentation 30+ / cosmetic ingredients) — each channel has a sub-host, using the same production system.
- Activate monetization funnel: premium subscriptions in-app, expert consultation packages, brand partnerships **according to "AI compatibility confirmed" standard** (clean revenue model, no selling out trust — reverse lesson from San Ge: over-aggressive behavior leads to channel bans and losing everything).
- **Milestones:** Matrix of 3–5 channels, $\ge$ 20K private community, referral $\ge$ 20% of new installs, first revenue streams from social running stably.

## 13. Risks & Mitigations

1. **Medical/legal risk** — skincare content interpreted as medical advice. $\rightarrow$ Doctor advisor approves all claims, standardized disclaimers on every video, do not discuss diseases requiring clinical treatment.
2. **"Fear-mongering" risk** — content team chasing views by scaring people. $\rightarrow$ Embed "no fear-mongering, no fabrication" (adhere to Minzhe International/Cao Gailun values) into mandatory guidelines, review weekly.
3. **Single face dependency risk** — host leaves, channel collapses. $\rightarrow$ Build founder IP + doctor pipeline in parallel + keep signature format belonging to the brand (audience identifies the format first, the person second).
4. **Platform risk** — algorithm changes, channel restricted. $\rightarrow$ Multi-platform matrix + private traffic funnel from month 1: followers belong to the platform, but the community belongs in our hands.
5. **Early ad spend burn risk** | spending paid ads before having winning formats. $\rightarrow$ Hard rule: first 3 months 100% organic, paid only replicates what has been proven.
